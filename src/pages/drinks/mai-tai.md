Combine all ingredients except dark rum into shaker with plenty of ice. Shake
vigorously. Strain into a rocks glass over crushed ice.

Float the dark rum over the top of the drink using the back of a spoon.

Slap the mint sprig to release its oils, then add it to the glass along with a
cocktail cherry as a garnish.
