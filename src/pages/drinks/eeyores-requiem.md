ingredients: [

  "½ oz London dry gin",
  "¼ oz Cynar",
  "¼ oz Fernet-Branca",
  "1 dash Angostura bitters",
  "1 dash orange bitters (Regan’s)",
]
garnish: 3 orange twists, discarded

---

Stir all ingredients with ice untl weill-chilled. Strain into a coupe.

Express the oil from two or three orange twists over the glass and discard the peels.
