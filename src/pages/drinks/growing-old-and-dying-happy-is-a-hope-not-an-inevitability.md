Add a small splash of absinthe to a coupe and swirl to coat the glass. Pour out the excess.

Add Cynar, rye, salt, and five lemon peels to a mixing glass with ice.
[Stir](/techniques/stirring/) until well-chilled (a regal stir—the peels express oils as you stir).
Strain into the prepared coupe.

Garnish with an additional lemon peel.
